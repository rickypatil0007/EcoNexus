11. Presentation Delivery Strategy
11.1 Overview

A successful EcoNexus demonstration depends not only on technical functionality but also on communication quality.

The presenter must deliver the product story in a way that connects:

Technical Innovation

        +

Real-World Impact

        +

User Experience

        +

Future Vision
11.2 Presenter Mindset

The presenter should not explain EcoNexus as a collection of features.

The presentation should communicate:

"We built a sustainability ecosystem
that helps people understand their impact,
take action, and create measurable change."
11.3 Delivery Principles
Principle 1 — Explain the Why Before the How

Incorrect approach:

"We used Three.js and Supabase..."

Correct approach:

"The challenge is that sustainability data
often feels disconnected from human actions.

EcoNexus solves this by making impact visible."

Technology comes after the problem is understood.
